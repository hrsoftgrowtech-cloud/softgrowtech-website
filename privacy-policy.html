Privacy & Data ProtectionSoftGrowTech

Privacy at a Glance

SoftGrowTech respects the privacy of applicants, interns and visitors. This policy explains what information may be collected through the website and how it is used to provide internship, training, support and document-verification services.

Information We May Collect

  • Name and basic application information.
  • Email address and registered mobile number.
  • Internship domain and program-related information.
  • Student / Letter ID and information required to verify an issued document.
  • Limited technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to process applications, communicate with applicants, administer internship and training activities, maintain program records, provide document verification and respond to support requests.

Public Verification

Public verification pages are designed to show only the information needed to confirm an issued record. Personal contact details may be masked or withheld from the public result.

Privacy approach: Only information reasonably needed for the requested service should be displayed publicly.

Data Protection

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect submitted information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or loss. As with any internet-based service, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Questions & Support

If you have a privacy-related question about information submitted through the website, please use the support/contact option provided by SoftGrowTech.
