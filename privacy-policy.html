Privacy Policy

Privacy & Data ProtectionSoftGrowTech

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through SoftGrowTech's website, internship application flow and document-verification services may be collected, used and protected.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Name and basic applicant information.
  • Email address and registered mobile number.
  • Internship domain, application and program-related information.
  • Student / Letter ID and information needed to verify an issued record.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to process applications, communicate with applicants, administer internship and training programs, maintain records, provide document verification and respond to support requests.

4. Public Verification Pages

Public verification results are designed to show only the information necessary to confirm an issued record. Personal contact details may be masked or withheld from the public result.

Privacy principle: We aim to display only information reasonably necessary for the stated purpose of the verification service.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or loss. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

6. Sharing of Information

Information is not intended to be publicly disclosed except where a website feature is specifically designed to provide a public result, where disclosure is required to operate a requested service, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Your Questions

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning information submitted through the website, contact SoftGrowTech through the support channel provided on the website.
