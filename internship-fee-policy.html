Internship & Training Fee Policy

Clear information about the one-time nominal fee, its timing, included program services and refund terms.

✓ Official Fee Information

Why is there a Nominal Fee?

The nominal fee supports the structured SoftGrowTech internship + training experience and the administrative, documentation and program resources provided throughout the journey.

There is no fee to apply

No payment is required when an applicant registers or submits an internship application. The fee is not a selection fee.

When does it apply?

After successful selection, an Orientation Session is conducted on the scheduled orientation start date. The applicable one-time nominal fee is communicated and collected after the Orientation Session.

What does the one-time fee include?

✓ Internship + training program participation
✓ Mentor guidance and doubt support
✓ Practical tasks, projects and evaluation
✓ Orientation, project presentation and training sessions
✓ Mock interview and career guidance
✓ Certificate processing and official record/document verification

One fee. No separate charges.

The applicable amount is a one-time nominal program fee. There is no separate fee for the included services listed in this policy.

NOTICE

Subject: Information Regarding Nominal Fee for Internship & Training Program

This notice is issued for the information of applicants and selected interns regarding the nominal fee applicable to the SoftGrowTech Internship & Training Program.

  1. No fee is required at the time of registration or while submitting an application.
  2. The nominal fee is not a selection fee and is not required to apply or to be considered for selection.
  3. After successful selection, an Orientation Session is conducted on the scheduled orientation start date. The applicable nominal fee is communicated and collected after the Orientation Session.
  4. The one-time fee covers the internship and training program together with the included mentoring, practical work, evaluation, presentation/training sessions, career guidance, certificate processing and official verification services.
  5. No separate fee is charged for the services included in the program. The applicable nominal fee is based on the participant's country/region.
  6. The nominal fee is non-refundable once paid.
