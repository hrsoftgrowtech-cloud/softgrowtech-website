Internship + TrainingNominal Fee Policy

Why Is There a Nominal Fee?

The applicable nominal fee is associated with the internship and training process and helps support the structured program experience, administration, learning activities, documentation and related services provided to participating interns.

Simple summary: It is a one-time nominal fee connected with the internship + training program, with the applicable program services covered under the same fee.

When Does the Confirmation Process Begin?

  1. Apply: You can submit your internship application through the normal application process.
  2. Orientation Session: After the applicable selection/onboarding stage, the Orientation Session is conducted. This session explains the program, learning structure, roadmap, expectations and next steps.
  3. Confirmation Process: After the Orientation Session, the confirmation process begins and the applicable nominal fee information is provided at that stage.

What Does the One-Time Fee Cover?

  • Internship and training program administration.
  • Structured learning and training support associated with the program.
  • Orientation and program-roadmap guidance.
  • Practical project and learning activities included in the applicable program.
  • Program documentation and related administrative support.

The above services are covered within the applicable one-time nominal fee where they form part of the selected program. No separate fee is intended for the same included service.

A Nominal, One-Time Contribution

The fee is kept at a nominal level for the applicable internship and training program. It is a one-time nominal fee, not a recurring charge.

Refund

The applicable nominal fee is non-refundable once paid, except where SoftGrowTech expressly confirms otherwise in writing for a specific case.

Important Note

The fee information is provided as part of the program's confirmation process. Applicants should read the relevant internship information and understand the orientation and confirmation steps before proceeding.
