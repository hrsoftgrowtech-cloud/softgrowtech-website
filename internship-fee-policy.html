Enrollment Fee Policy

Important: No fee before selection.Registration and assessment are free. The Enrollment Fee is payable only after you have been selected and are invited to enroll.

A clear guide to the nominal Enrollment Fee, why it applies, when it appears and how the enrollment process works.

SoftGrowTechOfficial Policy
RegistrationFree to apply and create your Student ID.
Nominal FeePayable only after selection.
✓Selection-Based ProcessEnrollment payment and selection are separate stages. Selection is determined by the assessment result.
Simple promise:You should always know what you are paying, why it applies, when it applies, and what happens if you are not selected.

1. Enrollment Fee Purpose

Initial registration and Student ID creation are free. The nominal, one-time Enrollment Fee is payable only after an applicant has been selected and invited to enroll. No Enrollment Fee is required before selection. It is an administrative/enrollment-stage charge for participation in the assessment process and associated processing; it is not a charge for the internship, certificate or selection.

Purpose of the fee: assessment participation and enrollment processing, participant record/documentation handling, verification, program coordination and related enrollment support. Payment does not purchase a selection decision, internship seat or certificate.

2. When the Enrollment Fee Applies

  1. Register: Create your Student ID and select your domain at no cost.
  2. Selection Assessment: Complete the assessment at no cost.
  3. Selection Review: The assessment is reviewed and the selection status is decided.
  4. Enrollment after selection: Only selected applicants are shown the applicable Enrollment Fee and payment methods. Do not pay before selection.

3. What the Enrollment Fee Supports

  • Selection assessment and enrollment processing.
  • Participant record and verification handling.
  • Program coordination and enrollment support.
  • Administrative processing connected with the selection stage.
  • Related participant support during the enrollment process.

The applicable amount and payment method are shown before payment. No Enrollment Fee is required to submit the initial registration, and the internship/certificate itself is not being sold through this fee.

4. Verification & Continuation

Payment verificationOnly after selection, the applicable enrollment payment may be submitted for manual verification. No payment is due before selection.
Before you pay

Review the amount and payment method shown on the official SoftGrowTech payment/enrollment screen. If anything is unclear, please contact the official support channel before submitting payment.
