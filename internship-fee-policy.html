Program Fee Policy

A transparent guide to the Enrollment Fee, when it appears and how the refund process works.

SoftGrowTechOfficial Policy

1. Enrollment Fee Purpose

Initial registration and Student ID creation are free. The Enrollment Fee becomes applicable only when an applicant reaches the enrollment stage of the Selection Assessment. It is an administrative/enrollment-stage charge for participation in the assessment process and associated processing; it is not a charge for the internship, certificate or selection.

Purpose of the fee: assessment participation and enrollment processing, participant record/documentation handling, verification, program coordination and related enrollment support. Payment does not purchase a selection decision, internship seat or certificate.

2. When the Enrollment Fee Applies

  1. Register: Create your Student ID and select your domain at no cost.
  2. Selection Assessment: Complete the website-based interview-style assessment when it becomes available.
  3. Enrollment: Before payment, the applicable Enrollment Fee and payment methods are displayed clearly. Payment details then enter verification while the assessment can continue.
  4. Selection Review: The assessment and application are reviewed and the selection status is updated by the SoftGrowTech team.

3. What the Enrollment Fee Supports

  • Selection assessment and enrollment processing.
  • Participant record and verification handling.
  • Program coordination and enrollment support.
  • Administrative processing connected with the selection stage.
  • Related participant support during the enrollment process.

The applicable amount and payment method are shown before payment. No Enrollment Fee is required to submit the initial registration, and the internship/certificate itself is not being sold through this fee.

4. Refund Policy

Refund for Non-Selection: If the participant is not selected, the applicable Enrollment Fee is refundable under the stated refund process and terms. The refund is initiated after the selection decision and is processed within 3 working days, subject to payment verification and the applicable program terms.
