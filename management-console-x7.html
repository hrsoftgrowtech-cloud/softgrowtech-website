Team Rules

Terms & Conditions

These rules apply to Team Members using the SoftGrowTech Management Console.

Access & Responsibilities

  • Use the Management Console only for work assigned by the Founder.
  • Keep login credentials confidential and do not share your account with anyone.
  • Access only the student, payment, task, document or business information needed for your assigned work.
  • Do not intentionally alter, export, disclose or delete sensitive records without authorization.
  • Do not attempt to access the Founder-only Activity Log, team management controls or other restricted areas.
  • Check your assigned permissions before making changes and report any suspected security issue to the Founder.

What Team Members Can Do

Your access is limited to the permissions assigned to your account. The available actions may include viewing or managing students, batches, tasks, assessment, payments, documents, support, enquiries, services or settings only where permission has been granted.

Account & Security

The Founder controls account access and permissions. Team Members cannot change their Management Console password. If access credentials are lost or need to be changed, contact the Founder.

Action for Misuse

Unauthorized access, credential sharing, deliberate data misuse, attempts to bypass permissions, or unauthorized changes may result in immediate suspension or removal of Management Console access and further action where appropriate.
