Before You Apply

One-Time Nominal Fee

A one-time nominal fee applies after selection and the scheduled Orientation Session. The fee covers the internship + training program and the included services — including mentor support, task & project evaluation, orientation and training sessions, project presentation, mock interview preparation, placement guidance, certificate processing and official document/record verification. No separate fee is charged for these included services.

No fee is required to submit the initial application. This is the only nominal program fee; no additional fee is charged for the included services. Check Nominal Fee ↗
