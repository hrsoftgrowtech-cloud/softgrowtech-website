FAQ / Q&A

Questions You May Have

Quick answers to the things most applicants want to know before starting.

What will I actually learn during the internship?

You’ll learn through practical tasks, domain-related projects, mentor guidance, orientation, project presentation and a final project designed to turn learning into real work.

Will I get mentor support while working on projects?

Yes. Mentor support is built into the learning journey to help with doubts, project direction, feedback and professional presentation of your work.

What makes this different from just watching tutorials?

The focus is on doing. You work on practical tasks and projects, present your work, receive feedback and build experience that can strengthen your portfolio.

Is there a fee for the internship?

A nominal one-time confirmation fee may apply after selection. Applicable program services are included, such as mentor support, task and project evaluation, orientation and presentation sessions, mock interview preparation, placement guidance, certificate processing and official document/record verification. There is no fee to submit the initial application.

What support do I get after completing my projects?

You can receive project feedback, presentation guidance, mock interview preparation and career or placement guidance to help you present your skills more confidently.
