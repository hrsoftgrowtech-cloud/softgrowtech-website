Create / Join Referral Program

Earn 30% reward on every successful registration, calculated from the applicable enrollment fee for the referred student’s country/region.

How it works

  • After joining, you receive a unique Referral ID and a personal referral link.

  • Rewards are calculated at 30% of the applicable enrollment fee. Eligibility and reward requests are subject to verification.
  • Do not use fake accounts, spam, misleading claims, or promise guaranteed selection, jobs, or earnings.
  • SoftGrowTech may review, reject, or reverse invalid or duplicate referrals according to program rules.

Terms & confirmation

By confirming, you agree to follow these rules and understand that creating a referral account does not guarantee that every referred registration will qualify for a reward. Reward eligibility is determined under the program rules and verification process.
